## English (en) — Primary Locale

**App Name (30 chars max):** GraceLog

**Subtitle (30 chars max):** Gratitude & Scripture Journal

**Promotional Text (170 chars max):** Cultivate daily gratitude with mood-tagged KJV Bible verses. Private, offline, and beautifully simple. Build a streak. Reflect. Grow.

**Description (4,000 chars max):**

GraceLog is a private, offline gratitude journal designed to help you cultivate thankfulness through daily reflection and scripture.

**How it works:**
- Each day, record what you are grateful for.
- Select your current mood — peaceful, thankful, joyful, hopeful, anxious, worried, or tired.
- Receive a carefully chosen King James Bible verse matched to your emotional state.
- Build a daily streak and celebrate milestones at 7, 30, 100, and 365 days.
- Review your week with mood trends, category breakdowns, and exportable summaries.

**Key Features:**
- **Completely Offline:** No accounts, no cloud sync, no data harvesting. Your journal lives on your device.
- **500 Mood-Tagged KJV Verses:** Scripture curated across seven emotional states, always available without internet.
- **Streak Tracking:** Visual flame milestones to build and maintain a daily gratitude habit.
- **Weekly Review:** Export your week as JSON, a 1080x1080 shareable image, or PDF.
- **Bedtime Mode:** A simplified OLED-black reflection screen for low-light gratitude entries.
- **Biometric Lock:** Optional Face ID / fingerprint protection for your journal.
- **11 Languages:** Full localization in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and Hebrew — with full RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew.
- **Accessible by Design:** Screen-reader friendly, 48dp touch targets, Dynamic Type support up to 310%, and Reduce Motion compliance.

**Pricing:**
GraceLog is free to use. A voluntary subscription of $0.99 per month removes banner advertisements. The subscription does not unlock any additional features — all journaling, scripture, streak, and export capabilities are available to every user.
