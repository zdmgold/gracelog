Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

GraceLog is designed to respect your privacy. Your gratitude journal, mood data, and scripture selections are stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate a backend server, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

GraceLog does not require user accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information. The app functions entirely offline. The following data never leaves your device:

  • Your daily gratitude entries and journal text
  • Mood selections and streak history
  • Scripture verses you bookmark or share
  • Weekly summary exports
  • Biometric lock preferences and bedtime mode settings
  • App theme and language preferences

2. Local Storage Only

All data is persisted locally using SQLite (via the sqflite package) and SharedPreferences (via the shared_preferences package) within your device's sandboxed application storage. You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage through your device settings.

3. Advertising (AdMob)

GraceLog displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob to support free usage. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). A single in-app subscription removes all banner ads permanently.

We do not share any journal content, mood data, or personal entries with AdMob or any third-party advertising network.

4. In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed securely by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). GraceLog does not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive a non-identifying transaction receipt from the platform to confirm your subscription status.

5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS Only)

On iOS 14.5 and later, GraceLog requests permission to access your device identifier for advertising purposes via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. This permission is optional. Denying it will not affect app functionality; you will simply see non-personalized ads.

6. Biometric Authentication

If you enable the optional biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID on iOS, fingerprint on Android), your biometric data is handled exclusively by your device's operating system. GraceLog never accesses, stores, or transmits biometric data.

7. Local Notifications

If you enable streak reminders, GraceLog schedules local notifications on your device. These notifications are generated and delivered locally without any server communication. You can disable reminders at any time in Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

GraceLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will assist in removing it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, retention is entirely under your control. Uninstalling the app deletes all associated data. There is no remote data to delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or app store update notes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@gracelog.app.
