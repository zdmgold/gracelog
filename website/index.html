Why GraceLog?

Completely Offline

Your journal lives on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no data harvesting. 500 mood-tagged KJV verses are bundled inside the app.

Mood & Scripture

Log how you feel — peaceful, thankful, joyful, hopeful, anxious, worried, or tired — and receive a matching Bible verse for reflection.

Streak Tracking

Build a daily gratitude habit with flame-streak milestones at 7, 30, 100, and 365 days. Gentle reminders keep you on track.

Weekly Review

Export your week as a beautiful summary. Share your blessings with an accountability partner or save them as an image.

Bedtime Mode

A simplified OLED-black reflection screen for late-night gratitude entries with reduced eye strain.

Accessible by Design

Screen-reader friendly, 48dp touch targets, Dynamic Type support up to 310%, and full RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew.
